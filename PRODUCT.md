# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

TechZone serves demanding gamers in Brazil who are comparing performance-oriented peripherals, hardware, and setup equipment. They need to assess products quickly, trust the store, and complete a purchase without feeling that they are interacting with a demo or study project.

## Product Purpose

TechZone is a premium ecommerce storefront for gaming technology. Its purpose is to help customers discover, compare, and buy equipment for high-performance setups with clarity, confidence, and a polished retail experience.

## Brand Personality

Premium, precise, and confident. The experience should feel engineered and refined, not noisy, childish, or generic.

## Anti-references

- Razer's brand language may inform the performance-led confidence, but TechZone must not copy its logo, neon-heavy identity, or visual assets.
- Avoid study, testing, debugging, fake-payment, and implementation-oriented language in customer-facing UI.
- Avoid generic neon-gamer templates, excessive visual effects, and SaaS-style card grids.

## Design Principles

1. Make product quality tangible through strong photography, clear specifications, and intentional hierarchy.
2. Earn trust at every purchase decision with concise delivery, warranty, and return information.
3. Let graphite, light space, and restrained technical green communicate premium performance.
4. Keep the path from discovery to checkout direct and calm.
5. Treat responsive and accessible behavior as part of the product, not a later enhancement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard-access requirements. Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide meaningful image alt text, and preserve usable layouts at mobile widths.
